# AI-Generated AGI Architectures Research Packet

## Overview
This research packet contains collected AGI architecture proposals from multiple AI systems, created as part of the bounty program for Cognitive-OS. The goal is to provide an auditable collection of different architectural approaches that can be compared and used for Cognitive-OS planning.

## Collection Method
We used standardized prompts across 8 distinct AI systems to collect AGI architecture proposals. The prompts were kept as consistent as possible across systems, with minimal adaptations when necessary for system-specific capabilities.

## Headline Findings
- Common themes across all proposals include modular design, multi-agent systems, and hierarchical organization
- Most proposals emphasize the importance of memory systems and learning mechanisms
- There's significant variation in approaches to embodiment and physical interaction
- Safety and alignment mechanisms appear in all proposals but with different implementation strategies

This research packet is intended to inform the development of Cognitive-OS by providing a comprehensive view of current thinking in AGI architecture from multiple AI perspectives.
