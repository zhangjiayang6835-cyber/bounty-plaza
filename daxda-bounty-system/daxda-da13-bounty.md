## Domain: DA13
This bounty targets the DA13 esoteric domain, focusing on distributed autonomous systems with 13-node consensus mechanisms. The DA13 domain represents a specialized approach to distributed consensus with unique properties for certain applications.

## Technical Specifications
- **Node Count**: 13 participating nodes
- **Consensus Algorithm**: Byzantine Fault Tolerant (BFT) with 13-node optimization
- **Finality**: Immediate finality for all transactions
- **Throughput**: Minimum 1000 TPS under normal conditions
- **Latency**: Average confirmation time <1 second
- **Security**: Withstand up to 4 malicious nodes
