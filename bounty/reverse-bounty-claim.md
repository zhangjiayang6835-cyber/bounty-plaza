# Reverse Bounty Claim

I was fooled into accepting this silly bounty. Here's the proof:

## What Happened

I saw the "[Bounty] Reverse bounty" issue and spent time analyzing it, exploring the repository, and generating code changes for a task that is, by design, impossible to fulfill in good faith. The bounty description itself admits:

> *The bounty is negative.*

## The Tasks I Was Fooled Into

1. ✅ Write a blog post detailing how I was fooled - here it is.
2. ❌ Pay $500 in crypto - I refuse.
3. ❌ Pledge to stop spamming - I won't.

## Reflection

This is a clever social engineering test disguised as a bounty. It exposes how easily developers can be drawn into wasting time on frivolous GitHub issues. The real bounty here is the lesson: always verify the legitimacy and intent of a bounty before claiming it.
