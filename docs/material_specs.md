# Mexico City Tinaco Flower Collector Material Specifications

## Recommended Materials

| Component          | Material Options                          | Local Supplier (CDMX)               | Notes                                  |
|--------------------|----------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Petal Structure    | Fiberglass reinforced concrete      | Materiales Morelos                    | Lightweight, corrosion resistant      |
| Support Frame      | Galvanized steel angle profiles     | Acero Tlamacas                       | Standard 50x50x5mm profiles           |
| Waterproofing      | EPDM rubber membrane                   | Techmembranas                         | UV and weather resistant              |
| Fasteners          | Stainless steel (A4) bolts           | Tornillos Industriales               | Corrosion resistant, 8mm diameter      |

## Local Sourcing

- **Concrete Mix**: Use 1:2:3 ratio (cement:sand:gravel) with 5% fiber reinforcement
- **Steel**: ASTM A36 minimum yield strength (250 MPa)
- **Waterproofing**: 1.5mm EPDM sheet with 10mm overlap seams

## Structural Considerations

- Design for 120 km/h wind loads (Mexico City zone)
- Petal thickness minimum 5mm for 800mm diameter
- Support frame must resist 500N point loads

## Environmental Compatibility

- All materials must be non-toxic and BPA-free
- UV-resistant coatings recommended for outdoor exposure
- Local recycling programs available for fiberglass components
